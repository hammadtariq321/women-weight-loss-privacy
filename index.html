Privacy Policy

Women Weight Loss · Effective date: November 12, 2026

In short: Women Weight Loss does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

1. Who we are

Women Weight Loss (“the app”) is a free fitness app published by Mehran Abbas (“we”, “us”). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at frontenddeveloper0123@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter in the app

To build your workout plan and show your progress, the app asks for some information. All of it is optional or editable:

  • A first name or nickname (optional)
  • Age, height, current weight and target weight
  • Fitness goal, focus areas, fitness level, activity level, workout preferences and areas to go easy on (for example, knees or lower back)
  • Workout history, weight log entries, “how did it feel” feedback and challenge progress
  • App settings and workout reminder times

This information is stored only on your device, in the app’s private local storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no way to see it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require or offer sign-up or login.
  • We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data, because we don’t receive any.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show the workout reminders you create. The app asks for this permission only when you turn on a reminder. You can turn it off any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): lets your reminders keep working after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: short vibrations to signal the start and end of exercises. You can turn this off in the app under Me → Voice & sound.

The voice coach uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The spoken text (for example, exercise names and countdowns) is generated by the app and is not recorded or sent anywhere by us.

5. Deleting your data

You are in full control. You can delete everything the app has stored at any time:

  • In the app: Me → Reset all data, or
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

Device backups (for example, Android’s own backup service) are managed by your phone and Google under their own privacy terms.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. As described above, the app does not collect personal data at all.

7. Health information disclaimer

The app provides general fitness guidance and estimates (such as calories burned, BMI and projected goal dates). It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Please talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you are pregnant, postpartum, or have an injury or health condition.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change means data would leave your device, we will tell you in the app before it happens.
